Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What documents do I need for ITR filing?
For salaried individuals: Form 16, Form 26AS, AIS, bank statements, investment proofs (80C/D), and home loan certificate if applicable. For business owners, additional P&L statements, GST returns, and balance sheet are needed. We send you a complete customised checklist after the first call.
What is your fee structure for ITR filing?
Our fees are transparent and fixed. Salaried individuals start from ₹1,500. Business owners start from ₹4,000 depending on complexity. Corporate filings start from ₹15,000. You receive a written quote before any work begins — no surprises.
How does the Client Portal work?
Once you become a client, we create a secure portal account for you. You upload documents, we process them, update status in real-time, and you can download all completed returns, certificates, and reports. All communication history is stored there too.
Can you help if I've received a tax notice?
Absolutely. We handle income tax notices (143(1), 148, 148A, 263), GST show-cause notices, and departmental audits. Share the notice with us immediately — early response always improves outcomes significantly.
Do you serve clients outside Delhi?
Yes. While our office is in Connaught Place, New Delhi, we serve clients pan-India remotely. All work is done digitally through our client portal — clients in Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and overseas NRIs are actively served by us.
How quickly can you register my company?
A Private Limited Company can typically be incorporated in 7–12 working days, subject to MCA processing time. LLP takes 5–8 days. We handle DSC, DIN, name approval, MOA/AOA drafting, and all MCA filings — you just need to sign digitally.
What's included in the free first consultation?
The first consultation is a 30-minute call or in-person meeting where we understand your situation, identify key issues, and recommend an approach. We provide an overview of what services you need and a written fee proposal. No commitment required from you.
